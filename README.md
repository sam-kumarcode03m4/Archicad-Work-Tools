## 🖥️ System Requirements

Before you begin, verify that your computer meets these hardware needs. ArchiCAD requires significant resources to render models smoothly.

* **Operating System:** Windows 10 (64-bit) or Windows 11.
* **Processor:** Intel Core i7 or AMD Ryzen 7 processor or better.
* **Memory:** 16 GB RAM minimum, 32 GB RAM recommended.
* **Graphics Card:** Dedicated GPU with at least 4 GB VRAM and DirectX 11 support.
* **Storage:** 10 GB of available space.
* **Display:** 1920x1080 resolution or higher.

> **Note:** Close all open programs before you start the installation to ensure the setup process completes without errors.
